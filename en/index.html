Imagined worlds,
brought into daily life.

RealRain is an IP commercialisation platform built for open creation. We publish through consumer goods: characters and works are licensed, developed and produced into things people can buy, on one distribution network shared by rights holders, creators and consumers.

How it works↓ Work with us

How IP gets published

Consumer goods, the new publishing medium.

A publisher prints a work as a book. RealRain publishes IP as products: within the scope of a licence, characters and works are developed into merchandise and branded goods, then manufactured and released to consumers.

  1. 01Rights holders

    Canon and rights

    Characters, worlds, works and assets are recorded together with what may be licensed and how revenue is shared.

    RainOntology · RainHub

  2. 02Creators · Brands

    Licensed development

    Content and products are developed from the canon and assets, inside the licence. Every derivative work is recorded.

    RainStudio

  3. 03Manufacturers

    Production

    Manufacturers join by category, process and delivery capacity, and fulfil through OEM runs or print on demand.

    OEM · POD

  4. 04Consumers

    Release

    Products are shown and released on RainFair. What consumers buy and tell us adjusts the direction of the next run.

    RainFair

Open creation, governed commercialisation.

AI has widened what can be developed from an IP, and made the rights around it more tangled. Creators work with whatever tools and communities they choose; once a work goes commercial, scope of use, review responsibility and revenue rules are made explicit first.

  • Product releaseRights holders

    Turn characters and works into products people can buy

    Rights holders can host standard merchandise on RainFair. The platform organises showcase, release and production, and adjusts product direction on what consumers buy and say.

  • Licensed co-creationCreators

    Help develop an IP and take the work commercial

    Within the licence, creators develop content and products from an IP's canon and assets. The platform records each work and its derivation, and sets out commercial use and revenue rules.

  • Brand licensingBrands

    Bring an IP into branded goods and new categories

    Brands license from rights holders around their own audience, categories and channels, put characters and works on products, and coordinate the release that follows.

  • ProductionManufacturers

    Become a standard supplier to RealRain

    Manufacturers join by category, process and delivery capacity, and produce through OEM or print on demand for products released on the platform.
