Crossroads 1.030 September 2026

Crossroads, a platform for developing and releasing IP.

Crossroads is the online platform RealRain runs. Rights holders build and run their IP there, creators develop content and merchandise under licence, and goods are released in the platform's market.

  • IP spacesCharacters, worlds, assets and works for each IP in one place, with versions and publishing status on record.
  • Licensed co-creationCanon and assets are published by version with a creative licence; work made under it keeps its source and credits.
  • WorkshopDerivative content, and physical merchandise such as badges, designed from an IP's assets.
  • MarketMerchandise is shown and released in the market, where consumers find goods through characters and works.

How IP gets published

Consumer goods, the new publishing medium.

A publisher prints a work as a book. RealRain publishes IP as products: within the scope of a licence, characters and works are developed into merchandise and branded goods, then manufactured and released to consumers.

  1. 01Rights holders

    Canon and rights

    Characters, worlds, works and assets are recorded together with what may be licensed and how revenue is shared.

    Crossroads · IP space

  2. 02Creators · Brands

    Licensed development

    Content and products are developed from the canon and assets, inside the licence. Every derivative work is recorded.

    Crossroads · Workshop

  3. 03Manufacturers

    Production

    Manufacturers join by category, process and delivery capacity, and fulfil through OEM runs or print on demand.

    OEM · POD

  4. 04Consumers

    Release

    Products are shown and released in the Crossroads Market. What consumers buy and tell us adjusts the direction of the next run.

    Crossroads · Market

  • Original IPOriginal creators

    Turn original characters and works into products

    Original creators can have RealRain publish their characters and works. RealRain organises product development, production and release, with rights and revenue shared as agreed.

  • Merchandise hostingRights holders

    Turn characters and works into products people can buy

    Rights holders can host standard merchandise in the Crossroads Market. The platform organises showcase, release and production, and adjusts product direction on what consumers buy and say.

  • Licensed co-creationCreators

    Help develop an IP and take the work commercial

    Within the licence, creators develop content and products from an IP's canon and assets. The platform records each work and its derivation, and sets out commercial use and revenue rules.

  • Brand licensingBrands

    Bring an IP into branded goods and new categories

    Brands license from rights holders around their own audience, categories and channels, put characters and works on products, and coordinate the release that follows.

  • ProductionManufacturers

    Become a standard supplier to RealRain

    Manufacturers join by category, process and delivery capacity, and produce through OEM or print on demand for products released on the platform.
