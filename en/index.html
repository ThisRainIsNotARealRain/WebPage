01 / HOW IP GETS PUBLISHED

Consumer goods, the new publishing medium

A publisher prints a work as a book. RealRain publishes IP as physical goods: within a licence, characters and works become merchandise and branded products, manufactured and released to consumers.

01 · Rights holders

Canon and rights

Characters, worlds, works and assets are recorded together with licensing scope and revenue rules.

02 · Creators & Brands

Licensed development

Content and goods are developed from canon and assets inside the licence, with every derivative work on record.

03 · Manufacturers

Production

Manufacturers join by category, craft and capacity, fulfilling through OEM runs or print on demand.

04 · Consumers

Market release

Products launch in the Crossroads Market, where purchases and feedback guide the next edition run.

Open creation, governed commercialisation

Creators work with whatever tools and communities they choose; once a work goes commercial, scope of use, review responsibility and revenue rules are made explicit first.

02 / CROSSROADS PLATFORM · 1.0

Crossroads, a platform for developing and releasing IP

Crossroads is the online platform run by RealRain. Rights holders build and manage their IP there, creators develop content and merchandise under licence, and goods are released in the platform market.

01 · IP Spaces

Characters, worlds, assets and works in one place, with versions and publishing status on record.

02 · Licensed Co-creation

Canon and assets publish by version with a creative licence; derivative work keeps source and credit.

03 · Workshop

Design derivative works and configure physical merchandise such as badges, cards and standees.

04 · Market

Merchandise is showcased and released in the market, connecting fans from works directly to goods.
