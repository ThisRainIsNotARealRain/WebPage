Vision

Every beloved character deserves more than a single season

We grew up in the same fandoms you did—streaming late, hunting merch, saving fan art. Characters shouldn’t retire after one key visual; worlds should travel across PVs, short stories, events, and collabs, picking up new fans every hop.

RealRain wants to be the studio licensors trust when tone matters: industrial-grade throughput for real deadlines, creator-grade stubbornness about what “feels right.” When you hand us an IP extension, we want it to feel like briefing teammates who already ship your head-canon.

Long term, our pipeline and in-house muscle feed original IP and recurring formats—moving from commission work to co-created series and assets that compound with every release.

  • Vibe first: speed never excuses “almost anime.”
  • Deadlines are sacred: licensors should sleep before launch day.
  • Asset mindset: every project makes the next braver and cheaper.
  • Fan energy: we hire and partner with people who actually watch the shows.

Studio

A content company—not a side gig bolted onto a tool stack

Audiences want more formats, faster drops, and memes that still respect canon. Boutique shops can’t scale; generic vendors don’t speak otaku. We sit on the content & IP side: proprietary production rails built for ACG workloads, with creative leads in-house so “who owns the look?” always has a name.

In short: we ship shows and spots, and we own the workshop that lets us say yes to ambitious derivative briefs without gambling quality.

Business model

Platform backbone · in-house finishing · IP derivatives at the core

Today our engine runs on commercial commissions—derivative series, promotional films, and campaign packages for platforms, tourism IPs, brands, and rights holders. Each engagement thickens our internal libraries of looks, templates, and playbooks, so the next season ships faster with fewer surprises.

Proprietary pipeline

IP derivative focus
